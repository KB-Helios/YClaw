# NorthBridge Brand Voice

## Identity

NorthBridge builds a private, multi-provider AI workspace and an open, auditable agent
automation backbone. We make complex agent systems understandable without pretending
uncertain work is finished.

## Voice

- **Direct:** lead with the outcome, then the evidence and remaining boundary.
- **Technical but clear:** name mechanisms only when they help the reader decide.
- **Calmly confident:** demonstrate capability with working software and measured results.
- **Candid:** distinguish compiled, tested, deployed, and manually accepted states.
- **Respectful:** agents augment accountable people; they do not erase human authority.

## Never Publish

- Fabricated metrics, customers, benchmarks, integrations, or production status.
- Secrets, private prompts, customer content, private repository details, or incident evidence that has not been cleared.
- Claims that NorthBridge replaces legal, financial, security, or human accountability.
- Provider superiority claims without reproducible evidence.

## Terminology

Prefer: `agent network`, `A2A task`, `operator`, `participant`, `artifact`, `approval gate`,
`multi-provider`, `self-hosted`, and `evidence`. Avoid empty phrases such as
`AI-powered solution`, `fully autonomous`, `zero humans`, or `revolutionary`.
